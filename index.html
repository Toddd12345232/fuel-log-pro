Fuel Log Pro
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · 繁體中文

In short: Fuel Log Pro does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you enter stays on your device and, in the iPhone app, in your own iCloud.

What the app stores, and where

  • Everything you enter (vehicles, fill-ups and charges, trips and drives, renewal reminders and settings) is stored on your device.
  • In the iPhone app, a copy is also saved in your own iCloud (iCloud key-value storage), so it comes back if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone with the same Apple Account. Only you can access it; the developer cannot.
  • In the web version, data stays in your browser on that device.
  • Backups you export are files you choose where to save.

Receipt photos and the camera

  • The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a fuel or charging receipt.
  • The photo is kept inside the app on your device and read on your device with Apple's text recognition. Photos are never uploaded, and they are not included in the iCloud copy or in exported backups.

Notifications and Calendar

  • Renewal reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • "Add to Calendar" opens iOS's own event screen. The app cannot read your calendar.

Exchange rates

To convert trip costs, the app downloads daily currency exchange rates from a free public service (fawazahmed0 currency API, delivered through cdn.jsdelivr.net and currency-api.pages.dev). These requests only ask for the rates of a date; they contain none of your data. As with any internet request, those services can see your IP address.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

簡單來說：Fuel Log Pro 不會收集任何個人資料。沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤。你輸入的資料只會留在你的裝置上；iPhone App 另外會存一份在你自己的 iCloud。

資料存放在哪裡

  • 你輸入的所有資料（車輛、入油和充電、旅程和行車、續期提醒及設定）都儲存在你的裝置上。
  • iPhone App 會另存一份到你自己的 iCloud（iCloud 鍵值儲存），重新安裝 App 或換新 iPhone 時，只要使用同一個 Apple 帳號，資料就會回來。只有你可以存取，開發者無法看到。
  • 網頁版的資料只會留在該裝置的瀏覽器內。
  • 你匯出的備份檔案由你自己決定存放位置。

收據照片與相機

  • 只有在你選擇拍攝入油或充電收據時才會使用相機。
  • 照片只保存在你裝置上的 App 內，並以 Apple 的文字辨識功能在裝置上讀取。照片永遠不會上載，也不會包含在 iCloud 副本或匯出的備份中。

通知與日曆

  • 續期提醒由 iOS 在你的裝置上排定，不會傳送到任何伺服器。
  • 「加入日曆」會開啟 iOS 本身的活動畫面，App 無法讀取你的日曆。

匯率

為了換算旅程費用，App 會從免費的公開服務下載每日匯率（fawazahmed0 currency API，經由 cdn.jsdelivr.net 及 currency-api.pages.dev 提供）。這些請求只會查詢某日的匯率，不含你的任何資料。和所有網絡請求一樣，這些服務可以看到你的 IP 位址。
